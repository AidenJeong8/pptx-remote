How it works

Three steps. About ten seconds.

Drop your deck

Open this page on the computer you're presenting from and drop in a .pptx. Your slides appear right away.

Scan the QR code

Point your phone's camera at the code. The remote opens in your phone's browser, so there's no app to download.

Present

Click Present fullscreen. From then on, everything happens from your phone.

Jump to any slide

Every slide is a thumbnail on your phone. Tap one and the big screen goes straight there.

Speaker notes

The notes from your .pptx show under the current slide. Only you can see them.

Private by design

The deck goes directly from your computer to your phone over an encrypted connection. It's never uploaded anywhere.

FAQ

Good to know

Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No, it usually connects over any network. If it can't, which can happen on some cellular or locked-down office networks, put both devices on the same Wi-Fi and it'll connect.

Do animations and transitions work?

No. Each slide shows in its finished state, with all builds already applied.

My fonts look a little different

Slides are drawn by your browser, so fonts that aren't embedded in the file and aren't installed on your computer get replaced with similar ones.

Why do I have to click on the computer to go fullscreen?

Browsers only let a page go fullscreen after someone clicks on that computer. Click once, and the phone handles everything after that.

Can I use Keynote or Google Slides?

Yes. Export to PowerPoint first: in Keynote, File → Export To → PowerPoint; in Google Slides, File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx).

Does it work on Windows?

Yes. The presenter works in any modern browser, on a Mac, a Windows PC or a Chromebook.
